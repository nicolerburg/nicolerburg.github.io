Photo framing: the two previews use the same proportions as the real website. Photo fit can either fill the frame or show the whole image. Left / right and Up / down choose the focus point, and Zoom lets you crop in a little more. These choices are saved separately for the gallery and profile.

Collection status: Active toys stay in the normal gallery. Lost toys move to the Lost tab and can include a last known location. Toys marked In Our Memory move to the memorial tab and can include an end date.

On iPhone: use Save / share both files first. On supported Safari versions it opens the iOS share sheet, where you can choose Save to Files and put both files straight into Soft Toy Inbox in iCloud Drive. If your browser will not share both files together, use the two fallback buttons.
