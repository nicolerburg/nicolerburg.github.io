Add a soft toy

This helper does not change the live website by itself. It creates two tidy files you can save to your iCloud “Soft Toy Inbox” and import later on your Mac.

On iPhone: use Save / share both files first. On supported Safari versions it opens the iOS share sheet, where you can choose Save to Files and put both files straight into Soft Toy Inbox in iCloud Drive. If your browser will not share both files together, use the two fallback buttons. The filenames are matched automatically later, so you do not have to rename anything.
